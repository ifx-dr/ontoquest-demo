    • Digital Reference

    The Digital Reference originates from the Productive4.0 project, part of the Industry 4.0 movement, which aims to create a digital platform to connect industries and value chains using technologies such as the Semantic Web and ontologies. The model uses a Semantic Web approach to map complex supply chain networks in the semiconductor industry and other areas, organizing 1500 classes into 14 "lobes" that represent specific aspects of the supply chain and serve as knowledge bases for both humans and machines.
